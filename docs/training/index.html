Daily path

Four steps, every morning

1Action queue
→
2Open the MR
→
3Claim or follow
→
4Update · Save

Work overdue, UNDER NTP, on hold, due soon, then missed ETAs. Don’t start from a 2,000-row dump unless you are hunting.

Step 1

Action queue

Open Queue

Grouped by urgency. Claim puts your name on Assign to. Follow notifies you when someone saves or chats.

Then the list

Work orders is every MR. Filters start collapsed. Click a KPI on Dashboard to jump into the matching set.

Step 2–3

Open, claim, see colleagues

  • Click a row — or j / k then Enter.
  • Status, due date, supplier and PO sit in the summary strip.
  • If someone else has the same MR open, their name shows at the top.
  • If they saved first, you get a conflict — reload their values or overwrite with yours. Never two rows for the same identity.

Step 4 · items

One form: supplier × item × date

On the MR

  • Type the supplier — the list completes unique names.
  • Type the item they can provide.
  • Need date on the line.
  • Alt+Enter adds another vendor row.

Not on the MR

  • There is no “Add supplier” here.
  • New vendors live on Materials.
  • Duplicates like AAGE vs AAGE W.L.L. are folded to one name.

Together

Several people, several orders

@mentions

Type @ in remarks or chat. Usernames appear. They get notified.

Bulk

x ticks a row. Assign, status or the same remark on many MRs at once (cap 80).

PLACED

Needs a PO number. ON HOLD and CLOSED ask for a remark. Rules are configurable.

  • Ctrl/⌘+K command palette — jump to a page, WO, supplier or person.
  • The list search also matches item lines, not only the header description.

Backup

Excel and the database together

  • Every backup copies SQLite + Excel — Backup now and the night schedule.
  • Backup first dumps every database row into file.xlsx, then snapshots the pair.
  • Files live in backups/YYYY-MM-DD/ as a matching .xlsx and .db.
  • Download is a zip of that pair. Upload accepts .xlsx, .db, or a zip — then confirm Restore.
  • Pairs older than 30 days move to backups/archive/; archives are deleted after 6 months.

Restore

If something looks wrong

Paired snapshot

Restore rolls database and Excel. A pre-restore snapshot is taken first.

Excel-only leftover

Replaces file.xlsx only. Does not overwrite live history. Seed from Excel if you really want those rows.

Docker down does not delete host data/ or backups/. Commands: docs/RECOVERY.md · ./scripts/recover.sh

What's new · September 2026

What changed for you

Your own tabs

The approvals desk opens on To sign (managers), Sent to sign or Received signed — no more 800-row board. Dispatch still gets the full desk.

Pop-up notifications

A slip landing in your queue pops on screen — review & sign straight from the card. Remind re-pings the manager with your own message.

Signature on the PDF

The drawn signature prints on the PDF at corporate size (~80×28 mm). Signing locks the slip — window closes with a Signed & locked stamp.

Email + production nginx

Mail via Gmail (app password), Resend or SMTP. And the app now runs behind nginx — one port, rate-limited, TLS-ready.

Also new: every report downloads again (10 kinds × PDF/Excel/CSV, charts included), accounts step is now optional (off), and “IM WO” is simply MR everywhere.

What's new · for managers

How you sign a document

On screen

  • Open the slip, press Review & sign….
  • Step 1 of the window: the request and the actual PDF.
  • Step 2: draw your signature, pick who carries the slip.

Then decide

  • Sign & send — locks prices and suppliers, hands the slip to the person you picked, and the window closes with a Signed & locked stamp.
  • Return with changes — write what to fix; the technician resends it to you.
  • You get a pop-up the moment a slip lands in your To sign tab. Waiting on someone else? Remind with your own message.

What's new · email

Set up mail — Gmail, Resend or SMTP

Gmail (new)

Settings → Email → Provider Gmail. Enter the Gmail address + a 16-letter Google App password (myaccount.google.com → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords). Save, send a test — done.

Resend (preset)

Paste your re_… API key and send a test — works even without a domain (test mail lands in the account owner's inbox, labelled with the intended recipient). Add a verified domain to deliver to everyone.

What gets mailed

Slip received to sign and signed slip back from the manager — plus verification, resets, assign pings and @mentions, to verified addresses. No mail configured? The in-app inbox and on-screen pop-ups carry everything.

Do not

Never do this

  • Do not keep Excel open for write on file.xlsx while people save.
  • Do not delete data/app_config.json (column mapping).
  • Do not replace data/woms.db while the API is running.
  • Do not treat an Excel-only restore as a full rollback.
  • Do not invent a second unofficial notes file — SKILLS.md is the living spec.

Go-live

Before the team depends on it

  1. Change default passwords. Create named users.
  2. Turn the backup schedule on. Copy backups/ off-box.
  3. Run it the production way: docker-run.bat, or deploy/start_production.sh — nginx on one port. Add a TLS cert at nginx if it leaves the office LAN.
  4. Walk Guide → Start tour once per person.
  5. Practice one Backup now + Download on a test copy.
